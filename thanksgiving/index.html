Two dinners to choose between

Dinner is the thing to get right, and there are two good ways to do it. After that, a spa afternoon if people fancy it, and one room to book. Prices are the good version of each rather than the cheapest, since the point is a memorable few days. Click any photo for full size.

The one thing that actually matters

A comfortable, tasty, stress-free Thanksgiving dinner for eight, where none of the eight of us has to cook, serve, or clean up.

Planning around the pups

Five dogs between us. Mom's one and Sis's two are housetrained and could travel at a pinch — it's our two puppies that can't, so the whole crew stays put. Easy enough to plan around:

  • Dinner somewhere else. Five dogs and a long sit-down meal wouldn't be much fun for anyone, dogs included.
  • No rush to get back. They're happy in the pen for up to eight hours, so the day can run as long as it likes.
  • Distance only counts for where we sleep. One drive out and home again is easy. It's days of back-and-forth that would get old — and all three places below are within about two and a half miles.

The decisionWhere we eat

Two ways to do Thanksgiving dinner. They cost within about twenty dollars of each other, so this is genuinely a choice about which evening we'd rather have — not about money. Same details in the same order for both, so it's easy to compare.

The difference that actually matters

Not the money — they're twenty dollars apart. It's that the firehouse gives us the whole day and Vizcaya gives us dinner. Eight hours of somewhere that's ours, with a chef arriving partway through, is a different sort of Thanksgiving to three hours of proper plated service in a mansion. Both are good. They aren't the same thing.

Small thingA room for the two of us

Everyone else has a bed already, so this is just one room for two nights or three — not something to organise the trip around. Worth doing nicely though, and Vizcaya is the obvious candidate since we may well be eating there anyway.

Vizcaya, 2019 21st St — bookable like any B&B

$109–230 / night

An 1889 mansion with a Victorian tower, 6,500 sq ft, in Midtown about a mile and a half out. Rooms are a normal booking — ResNexus portal or phone, no event required. Continental breakfast, free parking, en-suite bathrooms with imported British tiles. For the two of us the pick is probably a Carriage House room, which has a fireplace and a soaking tub, or the Penthouse if we want the whole top floor.
