Where we eat Thanksgiving dinner

Three genuinely different ways to do this. Every photo below can be clicked to see it full size, and every place links straight through to its own page so you can look properly and make up your own mind. Have a browse and say what appeals.

The one thing that actually matters

A comfortable, tasty, stress-free Thanksgiving dinner for eight, where none of the eight of us has to cook, serve, or clean up.

Everything else is negotiable. All three options clear that bar — private chefs and restaurants both include serving and washing up as standard. So they don't really compete on the food. They compete on how long the day lasts and who's in the room. That's the actual choice.

Dogs — and why they change the maths

Good catch from Sis. If we all decamp somewhere out of town and the dogs can't come, that's an hour's round trip every single day just to look after them. Here is what that actually rules in and out:

  • Options A and B don't have this problem at all. Nobody moves out of their own house, so the dogs stay exactly where they always are.
  • Grand Island Mansion is the only genuinely far option — roughly 30 miles, 35 to 40 minutes each way. That is the hour a day. Everything else on this page sits within about three miles of East Sac, call it ten minutes.
  • Vizcaya does not allow dogs in its rooms — confirmed. It is only a mile and a half away though, so nipping back is a short hop rather than an expedition.
  • The Midtown Victorian house takes pets and is the closest thing here at 1.2 miles — the one option where the dogs simply come with us.

Option AA historic house, just for the day

Rent a period home by the hour and bring a chef into it. Both places give a discount on long bookings, so the plan would be to take the house for eight hours — late morning through the evening — rather than just a dinner slot.

Why these come last

A rented house still leaves us with a kitchen we have to staff ourselves and a dining room nobody has measured. The inns turn up with a kitchen team already in the building. Houses only win if we want the place completely to ourselves.

Before anyone gets attached to one

Nothing is booked and nobody has been rung yet. Every price here was published or listed online, not quoted to us. The same question hangs over all three options: does each place actually work on Thanksgiving Day, and what do they charge extra for it? Beds stay available for months. Kitchens on Thanksgiving do not — so the dinner is the bit to settle first.

Prices are already creeping up

Re-checked on 19 August: the Midtown Victorian moved from $1,118 to $1,158, and another house jumped over $100, in a single day. Nothing dramatic yet, but holiday pricing only travels one way. Treat these as a snapshot, not a promise.

One question for everyone

The dates are Wednesday 25 to Saturday 28 November, which is three nights. If people would rather carry on through Sunday that's four nights, and all the lodging prices change. Worth settling early.
