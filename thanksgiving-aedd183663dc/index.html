Three places, and they fit together

Narrowed down to the three worth actually pursuing. They aren't really competing — one is where we eat, one is where we stay, and one is somewhere to spend an afternoon. Click any photo for full size.

The one thing that actually matters

A comfortable, tasty, stress-free Thanksgiving dinner for eight, where none of the eight of us has to cook, serve, or clean up.

Planning around the pups

Five dogs between us. Mom's one and Sis's two are housetrained and could travel at a pinch — it's our two puppies that can't, so the whole crew stays put. Easy enough to plan around:

  • Dinner somewhere else. Five dogs and a long sit-down meal wouldn't be much fun for anyone, dogs included.
  • No rush to get back. They're happy in the pen for up to eight hours, so the day can run as long as it likes.
  • Distance only counts for where we sleep. One drive out and home again is easy. It's days of back-and-forth that would get old — and all three places below are within about two and a half miles.

Where we eatThe 1917 Firehouse

Front-runner

Hired for the day, with a chef brought in

~$1,224 + chef

Click any photo for full size — there are more on the listing.

A genuine 1917 fire station in Curtis Park, converted into a 3,700 sq ft home. The old engine garage is now a large kitchen-dining-living room with a backlit onyx bar, which means real room for a chef to work. The firepole is still there, along with craftsman woodwork, two sets of firehouse doors on the front, and a boy scout badge inlaid into the floor.

Since the dogs are fine penned for the day, take the full eight hours — the long-booking discount means late morning through the evening costs barely more than a dinner slot.

Where we stayVizcaya

An 1889 mansion with a Victorian tower and columned entrance, 6,500 sq ft, in Midtown at 2019 21st St — about a mile and a half away.

Book the rooms

Eight en-suite rooms, bookable like any B&B

$109–230 / night

The useful discovery: the rooms are a normal booking, through their ResNexus portal or by phone — no event required, unlike some places. Continental breakfast, free parking, en-suite bathrooms with imported British tiles throughout.

And there's a catch on top Their rate card says “food minimums in addition to facility fees will apply” without saying what they are. The cheapest route is the Coach House, which takes small events up to 40 people at $750 for three hours — even then it lands near $2,100 once food, drinks, the 22% service charge and tax are added. The firehouse plus a chef does the same job for less.

An afternoonThe Spa at Parkside

A boutique co-ed day spa inside the historic 1936 Fong Mansion downtown, about two and a half miles out. Open 10am–7pm daily.

Nothing is booked
