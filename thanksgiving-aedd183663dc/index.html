Where we eat Thanksgiving dinner

Three genuinely different ways to do this, all researched, all with links so you can look for yourself. Have a click through and say what appeals. Nothing has been booked and nobody has been called yet — this is for deciding, not confirming.

The one thing that actually matters

A comfortable, tasty, stress-free Thanksgiving dinner for eight, where none of the eight of us has to cook, serve, or clean up.

Everything else is negotiable. All three options below clear that bar — private chefs and restaurants both include serving and cleaning up as standard. So they don't really compete on quality of food. They compete on how long the day lasts and who's in the room. That's the actual choice.

Option AA historic house, just for the day

Rent a period home by the hour and bring a chef into it. Both places below give a discount on long bookings, so the plan would be to take the house for eight hours — late morning through the evening — rather than just a dinner slot. We'd sleep elsewhere.

Restored 1917 Firehouse

about $1,224 for the day

An actual 1917 fire station in Curtis Park, converted into a 3,700 sq ft home. The old engine garage is now a big kitchen-dining-living room with a backlit onyx bar — so it's the better choice if we care more about the chef having room to work than about the wallpaper.

The character is odd rather than elegant: the firepole is still there, along with craftsman woodwork, two sets of firehouse doors on the front, and a boy scout badge inlaid into the floor. Also has three bedrooms.

Option CAll of us under one roof, for three nights

The only option that gets us days together instead of hours, and the only one where Mom could stay too rather than driving home. The historic inns do lodging, venue and kitchen in a single booking.

Best of these

Vizcaya Sacramento

rates not published

An 1889 mansion with a Victorian tower and columned entrance, 6,500 sq ft, in Midtown. Eight en-suite bedrooms across the main house and carriage house, including a 900 sq ft penthouse suite — so everyone, Mom included, could sleep there.

The big advantage: they have their own kitchen team and build menus to order. No outside chef to organise, no strange kitchen, no asking permission to bring caterers in.

vizcayasacramento.com → 916-594-9285 frontdesk@vizcayasacramento.com
Still need to check There's no guest kitchen, so no making your own coffee or raiding leftovers over four days. Rates aren't published anywhere. The two questions that decide it: does their kitchen team work Thanksgiving Day, and can we book it as somewhere to stay without signing an event contract?

Before anyone gets attached to one

Nothing is booked and nobody has been rung yet. Every price here is one that was published or listed online, not a quote. The same question hangs over all three options: does each place actually work on Thanksgiving Day, and what do they charge extra for it? Beds stay available for months. Kitchens on Thanksgiving do not — so the dinner is the bit to decide first.

One question for everyone

The dates are Wednesday 25 to Saturday 28 November, which is three nights. If people would rather stay through Sunday that's four nights and all the lodging prices change. Worth settling early.
